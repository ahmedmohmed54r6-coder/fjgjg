# Find Phone (SMS)
Locate your phone by SMS: no server, no internet needed.

Setup (once): choose a secret word (6+ chars) and a lock PIN, grant permissions (location "all the time", Phone), disable battery optimization. The app then locks; settings need the PIN.

Use: from ANY phone, text the secret word to your lost phone. It replies with a Google Maps link (GPS works offline). Text "<word> ring" to make it ring.
Dual SIM: the reply tries the SIM that received the request, then the other SIM, retrying on no coverage.
